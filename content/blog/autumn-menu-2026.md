September means one thing in our kitchen: the game season opens and the menu gets heartier. Here's what's changed.

## Back on the menu

**Steak & Adnams ale pie.** It came off in May and we've had complaints ever since. It's back, with suet pastry and proper gravy.

**Roast partridge.** From the estate a few miles up the road, served with celeriac, blackberries and a bread sauce. Available Thursday to Saturday while it lasts.

## New this season

**Pumpkin & sage risotto.** Local squash roasted until sweet, folded through carnaroli rice with aged parmesan and crispy sage. Vegetarian, and one of the best things we've put on the menu this year.

**Crispy Suffolk pork belly** with burnt apple and black pudding crumb as a starter — or ask for a main-course portion.

## On the bar

Our guest ale rotation goes darker as the nights draw in. Look out for Woodforde's Nog and a chocolate porter from a Cambridge brewery in October.
