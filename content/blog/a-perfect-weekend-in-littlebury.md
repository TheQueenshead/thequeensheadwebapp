Littlebury is the kind of village people drive through on the way to somewhere else — and then wish they'd stopped. Here's how we'd spend 48 hours if we were staying with us.

## Saturday

**Morning.** Check-in isn't until 3pm, so arrive early, drop your bags with us and head straight to **Saffron Walden market**. It runs every Tuesday and Saturday on the Market Square and has been doing so, in one form or another, since 1141. Pick up a Colchester oyster or two, then wander the pargeted streets around Church Street.

**Lunch.** Back at the Queen's Head for our Saturday lunch menu — the beer-battered haddock is the regulars' choice.

**Afternoon.** Walk it off with the [Littlebury & Audley End circular](/about#walks): out along the River Cam to the edge of Audley End's parkland and back through the meadows. Three and a half miles, mostly flat, and you'll be back in time for a bath before dinner.

**Evening.** Dinner in the restaurant. Ask about the guest ale, and if the sticky toffee pudding is on (it usually is), don't skip it.

## Sunday

**Morning.** A lie-in, then breakfast until 10.30am. Then it's a mile down the road to **Audley End House**, which opens at 10am. Give yourself at least three hours — the service wing and the kitchen garden are as good as the state rooms.

**Lunch.** Our Sunday roast is served until 5pm, so there's no rush. Book before you go — Sundays fill up.

**Afternoon.** If you've time before the drive home, Bridge End Garden in Saffron Walden is free to enter and its hedge maze is more difficult than it looks.
